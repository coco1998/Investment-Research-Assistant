# Workspace Health

Use this checklist when the workspace starts to feel inconsistent.

- Every project has a `master.md`.
- Reports live under `projects/[Project Name]/reports/`.
- Meetings live under `workspace/meetings/` and include `project`, `company`, `date`, `type`, and `summary` frontmatter when possible.
- Cross-project knowledge lives under `knowledge/2_wiki/entities/` and `knowledge/2_wiki/concepts/`.
- Generated data such as `workbench/data.js` is not committed.
- Private source materials stay inside `workspace/`, which is ignored by Git.
